Our Story

Flowers Made with
Passion & Care

Flower Lab is a home-based floral studio nestled in Linksfield, Johannesburg. Every arrangement is made by hand, with fresh blooms carefully selected to suit your occasion, budget, and personal style.

What started as a love for flowers has grown into a small business built on trust, creativity, and the belief that flowers have the power to say what words sometimes can't.

We serve clients within a 15km radius of Linksfield, including Bedfordview, Edenvale, Sandringham, Houghton, Kensington, and surrounding areas.
