Investec vs RMB Private Account:
The Full 2026 Comparison

Both banks target high-net-worth South African professionals, but they differ meaningfully on fees, international banking, lounges, and the breadth of private banking services offered.

By the Bank Investec Editorial Team · Last updated: March 2026 · Not affiliated with Investec or FirstRand
Our Verdict
Investec wins for global professionals; RMB wins on fee flexibility

Both Investec and RMB Private Account are genuinely premium offerings targeting similar high-income South African clients. Investec edges ahead for professionals who travel frequently, transact internationally, or want a truly all-in-one private banking experience, with its multicurrency card, 1,100+ airport lounges, and the UK bank account add-on being clear differentiators.


RMB Private Account may suit clients who already bank with FNB and want to leverage their existing eBucks ecosystem, or those who can maintain a R200,000 account balance to waive the monthly fee entirely. RMB's integrated wealth management and lending suite is also highly regarded.


For most ambitious South African professionals starting their private banking journey, Investec's single transparent fee and superior international features make it the stronger choice, particularly if you earn above R800,000 and travel internationally with any regularity.
