FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before applying for Investec Private Banking.

How much does Investec Private Banking cost?
Investec charges one transparent all-inclusive monthly fee of R675 (effective 1 August 2025). Young Professionals under 30 pay R340/month. This single fee covers all core banking — no separate charges for local ATM withdrawals, EFTs, debit orders, or day-to-day transactions.
Do I qualify for Investec Private Banking?
You generally need a relevant professional qualification and a minimum annual income of R800,000 if you are over 30, or R600,000 if you are under 30. Specific professions and qualifications may allow for alternative eligibility. Apply via our link and Investec will assess your profile directly.
What is the Investec Rewards programme?
Investec Rewards has no membership fee and no cap on earning. You earn 1 point per R20 on qualifying card transactions and 1 point per R2,000 of qualifying balances. Points never expire and can be redeemed for cash, travel, investments, or British Airways Avios — in whatever combination suits you.
Can I transact internationally with Investec?
Yes. Investec's Multicurrency Pockets and Intelligent Routing™ technology lets you transact in pounds, dollars, euros, and rands using a single Investec Visa card — with automated real-time currency detection. You can also add a UK Private Bank Account (GBP 1,000 opening deposit required; the £10/month maintenance fee is waived if you hold an active SA Private Bank Account).
What are the Young Professional benefits?
If you are under 30 and earn R600,000+ per year with a relevant qualification, you qualify at R340/month. Young Professionals also receive R25,000 in complimentary life insurance, access to an online share trading account with no minimum and no annual admin fee, tax-free investment accounts, and exclusive insurance products via Aon South Africa.
Does Investec have physical branches?
Investec operates private banking suites (not traditional retail branches) in Johannesburg, Cape Town, and Durban. The model is digital-first via the Investec App and Online Banking, complemented by a dedicated private banker available 24/7 and supported by specialist teams across lending, wealth, and investments.
How do I apply for Investec Private Banking?
Click any "Apply Now" button on this page to go directly to our secure Investec referral link. You'll complete an online application with your personal, employment, and income details. Investec will assess your eligibility and a private banker will contact you to guide you through onboarding.
Is Investec a safe and reputable bank?
Yes. Investec Bank Limited is a registered South African bank regulated by the South African Reserve Bank (SARB) and the Prudential Authority. Investec has operated since 1974 and is dual-listed on the JSE and London Stock Exchange. It was named Bank of the Year 2025 by The Banker (Financial Times Group) and has been South Africa's Best Private Bank for 13 consecutive years.
